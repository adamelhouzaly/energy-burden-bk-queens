# Findings: Energy Burden in Brooklyn & Queens

_Data: U.S. Census ACS 5-year estimates (2018-2022). High burden = 6%+ of income._

- Analyzed **1,448** census tracts with usable data (of 1,530) and **32** neighborhoods.
- **24** tracts (about **24,046** households) are at or above the 6% affordability line.
- Brooklyn household-weighted average burden: **2.8%**
- Queens household-weighted average burden: **2.7%**
- Income vs. burden rank correlation (Spearman): **-0.47** (negative = lower-income areas spend a larger share on energy).

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize bill-assistance outreach (e.g., HEAP, utility discount programs) in the top-burden neighborhoods above.
2. Pair outreach with energy-efficiency and weatherization referrals where burden is high but incomes are just above program cutoffs.
3. Re-run this analysis each year when new ACS data is released to track progress.
